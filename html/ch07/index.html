Granola Energy Bites

Posted September 1, 2024 by Coach Riley

These no-bake granola bites are packed with oats, honey, and peanut butter for a quick energy boost before a hike. They take about ten minutes to mix and need an hour to chill. Roll them into balls, store them in the fridge, and grab a few whenever you need fuel on the trail. They keep for up to a week.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ¼ cup mini chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Stir all ingredients together in a bowl.
  2. Chill the mixture for 30 minutes.
  3. Roll into 1-inch balls.
  4. Refrigerate and enjoy within a week.
Nutrition facts (click to expand)

Per serving: 180 calories, 7g protein, 9g fat, 22g carbs.

Did you know?

Oats are a whole grain rich in fiber, which helps keep you full longer - perfect for long hikes.
